FUKURUMA

One galaxy, seven vehicles nested like Russian dolls, and every one of them walked on foot.

Play in the browser

About

Fukuruma is a space exploration game where the Commander travels a procedurally generated galaxy in a chain of nested vehicles: a 144 km Jump Ship carries Space Stations, which carry Continental Explorers, which carry Land Explorers, which carry Walkers, which carry the Commander's 3.6 m Exosuit. Each one is steered from its own cockpit, and each one can be walked through as a person, from the bridge to the suit bay to the hangar where the next doll waits.

Out on the worlds, danger is read through sensors: every doll sees only what its sensors resolve. What kills you is smaller than your vehicle can see, and what kills your vehicle is at least its own size. Natives trade and talk, ruins hide puzzles and guardians, and thieves take what you leave unattended. Survival comes first: scan, avoid, outsmart.

Features

  • Seven nested dolls with their own cockpits, controllers and rooms made for people.
  • A seamless descent from orbit to the ground: planets with atmospheres, clouds, oceans, rivers and weather.
  • Sensors with range and resolution, passive scans and active pings, and scan data that ages.
  • Survival: suit systems, toxic zones, radiation, predators, fire, Clone and Legacy modes.
  • Natives with dialogue and markets, ruins with puzzles, thieves and boarding parties.
  • Research, crafting, outposts, a codex that carries across Commanders, and a mystery to follow.
