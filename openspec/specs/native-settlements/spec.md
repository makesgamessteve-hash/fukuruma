# native-settlements Specification

## Purpose

Where the natives live: settlements on liveable worlds with buildings and walking natives, which are sensor signatures and meet the Commander according to their attitude.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Settlements of natives
Worlds with air and a liveable climate SHALL have settlements from the seed, with buildings and natives walking
among them. Natives SHALL be sensor signatures. Hostile natives SHALL attack a suit that comes close.

#### Scenario: A hostile settlement
- **WHEN** the suit stands among natives whose faction is hostile
- **THEN** spears hurt the suit
