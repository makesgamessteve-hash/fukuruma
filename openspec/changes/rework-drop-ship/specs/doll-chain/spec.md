## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Drop Ship carries a Continental Explorer
The Drop Ship SHALL be big enough to hold a Continental Explorer, and SHALL carry one in its hold. From its seat, the
Commander SHALL be able to set it down and to take aboard a Continental Explorer near it that has nothing out.

#### Scenario: Taking one aboard and setting it down
- **WHEN** a Continental Explorer stands near a Drop Ship and the Commander, at the Drop Ship's controls, recalls it
  and then deploys it
- **THEN** it goes into the Drop Ship's hold, and comes out again with the Drop Ship as its parent

#### Scenario: The way up
- **WHEN** a new game starts
- **THEN** the Drop Ship that brought the Continental Explorer down waits beside it, its hold empty
