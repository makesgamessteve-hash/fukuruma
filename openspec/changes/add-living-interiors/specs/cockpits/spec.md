## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Detailed cabins
Each cockpit SHALL have the following, with the lights drawn glowing:
- a seat with a pedestal, headrest and armrests;
- a sloped console;
- side consoles with lit button rows;
- an overhead panel;
- wall ribs, floor grating and strip lights.

#### Scenario: At the Walker's controls
- **WHEN** the Commander sits at the Walker's controls
- **THEN** the cabin shows its seat, consoles, buttons and screens
